[h2] From “dinner?” to “paid back” in one thread
[p] Ask
[h3] Say what you want and who's coming
[p] Text Rendezvous like you'd text a friend. It saves everyone's numbers, so next time "dinner with the roommates" is enough.
[p] No app to download. It's just iMessage.
[p] Plan
[h3] It finds the fairest spot
[p] It checks when everyone's free, what they can spend, and how far each person walks. Then it picks the place where the longest walk is shortest.
[p] Calendars share free/busy only. Balances and locations stay private.
[p] Agree
[h3] Everyone taps 👍, or asks for a change
[p] A reply like “can we do Indian instead” starts a new round in seconds. After three rounds, the organizer picks.
[p] Quiet friends get ten minutes before it locks in.
[p] Book
[h3] It calls the restaurant
[p] Grok Voice calls, says it's an AI, and books the table. When the host asks something only you can answer, the question lands in your chat.
[p] It never gives out a card number.
[h3] Everyone gets a nudge to leave
[p] Each person gets a text 15 minutes before they need to head out, timed to their own walk, with directions that open in Maps.
[p] Different routes, one arrival time.
[p] Settle
[h3] The bill splits itself
[p] Whoever paid says so. Each person taps 👍 on their own line, and only then does their money move, through Capital One Nessie.
[p] Nobody can pay on someone else's behalf.

[h2] It knows just enough
[p] Rendezvous
[p] takes the least it needs for each step, and nothing moves without the right person's say-so.
[dt] Calendars
[dd] It sees when you're busy, never what you're doing. Event names and guests stay on your calendar.
[dt] Location
[dd] Where you are only feeds the walking math. Nobody else in the plan sees it.
[dt] Balances
[dd] Your balance only rules out places you can't afford. Nobody in the chat ever sees it.
[dt] Payments
[dd] Money moves only when the person who owes taps 👍 on their own line. Anyone else's tap is ignored.
[dt] Phone calls
[dd] It says it's an AI at the start of every call, and it never speaks or stores a card or account number.
