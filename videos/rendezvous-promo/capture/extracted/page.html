Four routes,one table.

Rendezvous lives in iMessage. Text it what you want to do and who's coming. It picks the spot that's fairest to everyone's walk, calls to book the table, tells each of you when to leave, and splits the bill afterward.

Plan a dinner hereSee how it works

From “dinner?” to “paid back” in one thread

  1. 1Ask

    Say what you want and who's coming

    Text Rendezvous like you'd text a friend. It saves everyone's numbers, so next time "dinner with the roommates" is enough.

    No app to download. It's just iMessage.

  2. 2Plan

    It finds the fairest spot

    It checks when everyone's free, what they can spend, and how far each person walks. Then it picks the place where the longest walk is shortest.

    Calendars share free/busy only. Balances and locations stay private.

  3. 3Agree

    Everyone taps 👍, or asks for a change

    A reply like “can we do Indian instead” starts a new round in seconds. After three rounds, the organizer picks.

    Quiet friends get ten minutes before it locks in.

  4. 4Book

    It calls the restaurant

    Grok Voice calls, says it's an AI, and books the table. When the host asks something only you can answer, the question lands in your chat.

    It never gives out a card number.

  5. 5Go

    Everyone gets a nudge to leave

    Each person gets a text 15 minutes before they need to head out, timed to their own walk, with directions that open in Maps.

    Different routes, one arrival time.

  6. 6Settle

    The bill splits itself

    Whoever paid says so. Each person taps 👍 on their own line, and only then does their money move, through Capital One Nessie.

    Nobody can pay on someone else's behalf.

It knows just enough

Rendezvous takes the least it needs for each step, and nothing moves without the right person's say-so.

Calendars
It sees when you're busy, never what you're doing. Event names and guests stay on your calendar.
Location
Where you are only feeds the walking math. Nobody else in the plan sees it.
Balances
Your balance only rules out places you can't afford. Nobody in the chat ever sees it.
Payments
Money moves only when the person who owes taps 👍 on their own line. Anyone else's tap is ignored.
Phone calls
It says it's an AI at the start of every call, and it never speaks or stores a card or account number.

What it runs on

One agent server holds the plan and talks to the group through Photon. A separate Python bridge handles the phone audio. Built in Cursor at BigRed//Hacks 2026.

  • PhotonThe whole interface: iMessage threads and group chats, tapback approvals and departure texts.
  • GrokReads what the group asks for, and Grok Voice places the reservation call.
  • Capital One NessieIthaca restaurants as merchants, budgets from balances, and the transfers that settle the bill.
  • Google Maps and CalendarWalking times for every route, and free/busy for every calendar.
  • Twilio and PipecatBridge the restaurant's phone line to the voice agent.
  • GoDaddy Registrytablefor.us, because it's a table for us.
