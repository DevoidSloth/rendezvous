## Intent

Sell it: the four-routes story. Four friends leave from four places at four
different times and all arrive at one table at 7:15. Then the loop that makes
it happen: you text what you want and who's coming, it finds the fairest spot,
calls to book, texts each person when to leave, and splits the bill.
A ~30s promo for the tablefor.us site and the BigRed//Hacks 2026 Devpost entry.

## Customizations

- Keep the site's transit-map line-diagram identity: one colored line per friend,
  signal red for the table.
